What is CIAW?

The Center for Innovation in Ancient Worlds (CIAW) is an independent, non-profit academic initiative founded in 2025 and based in Catalonia, Spain. Bringing together Ancient History, Classics, Archaeology and Reception Studies, it promotes interdisciplinary research, scholarly publishing, educational innovation and public engagement with the ancient world.

Rather than operating as a single university department, CIAW provides a collaborative framework open to scholars, educators and cultural practitioners across institutions and countries. It explores not only what we know about Antiquity, but also how historical knowledge is produced, represented, taught and shared—from the critical study of ancient evidence to video games, digital humanities and emerging technologies.

Through its Diamond Open Access journal AI & Antiquity, the Playing Antiquity thematic series and its work on neurodiversity-aware teaching, CIAW is committed to rigorous scholarship, academic independence, accessible learning and the open exchange of ideas.

Archeion is CIAW's public outreach initiative, connecting academic research with broader audiences.

CIAW is rooted in academic collaboration in Catalonia, with close links to the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and colleagues across other institutions. Meet the people behind CIAW →

NEURODIVERSITY BY DESIGN

Different Minds. Better Humanities.

Neurodivergent students, educators and researchers contribute different experiences and perspectives to Ancient Studies. Inclusion is not simply a matter of offering individual accommodations; it means designing academic environments that value cognitive difference from the outset.

Same standards, different pathways. We preserve rigorous historical analysis while reducing unnecessary cognitive, sensory and social barriers to participation.
