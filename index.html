ABOUT CIAW

What is CIAW?

The Center for Innovation in Ancient Worlds is an independent, non-profit academic initiative founded in 2025. It connects scholars, educators and cultural practitioners interested in how the ancient world is researched, represented, taught and shared. Our approach combines scholarly rigour, interdisciplinary exchange, openness and accessibility.

FLAGSHIP PUBLICATION

AI & Antiquity

An international, peer-reviewed Diamond Open Access journal linking Ancient Studies with teaching, artificial intelligence, digital humanities and critical approaches to historical knowledge.

Open research, interdisciplinary dialogue

AI & Antiquity publishes scholarship on the ancient world and emerging technologies, inclusive education and new forms of cultural representation. Its thematic publications include Playing Antiquity, dedicated to video games and the ancient world.

RESEARCH AREA

Game Studies & Antiquity

Video games are more than illustrations or teaching aids: their narratives, rules and systems actively shape how we imagine and interpret the ancient world. CIAW approaches games as objects of scholarly research, not only as educational tools.

Classical reception

How games reinterpret historical events, societies, identities and cultural memory.

Mechanics & historical agency

How rules, simulation and player choices construct playable versions of the past.

Teaching & public history

Critical game literacy, digital heritage, educational practice and historical knowledge.

ANNUAL THEMATIC SERIES

Playing Antiquity

Video Games, Teaching and Historical Knowledge. This recurring series within AI & Antiquity welcomes research on games, classical reception, mechanics, historical interpretation, education and digital culture. Contributions are not required to address AI or classroom use.
