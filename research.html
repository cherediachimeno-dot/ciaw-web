Selected initiatives

Forgotten Voices in Antiquity (GI517492, 2025–2026) explores inclusive Ancient History teaching, historically marginalised groups, critical engagement with generative AI and accessible learning design. The articles and resources above document different stages of that work.

Inclusive AI Workshops develop approaches to verification, assessment and responsible technology use in humanities education. Archeion supports broader engagement with Ancient History and digital culture (Archeion on Instagram ↗).
