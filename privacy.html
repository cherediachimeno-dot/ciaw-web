Privacy & website information

This notice concerns ciaw.es, the informational website of the Center for Innovation in Ancient Worlds (CIAW), an independent, non-profit academic initiative founded in 2025 and based in Catalonia, Spain. CIAW's website and AI & Antiquity's journal platform are separate websites.

Contact and enquiries

The CIAW website has no account registration or contact form. If you contact us by email, your address and message are used to manage and respond to your enquiry. You may request access, correction or deletion of information you sent by writing to carlos.heredia@uab.cat.

Website hosting and third-party services

This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Technical access information may be processed by the hosting provider in the ordinary course of serving web pages. The main site requests typefaces through Google Fonts, which may receive technical connection information. External academic and social-media links lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

CIAW does not currently embed advertising, analytics trackers, or cookie-based profiling scripts on this website. Third-party services accessed through outgoing links are subject to their own policies.

Independent status and academic links

CIAW is an independent academic initiative. Its collaborators may hold appointments at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) or other institutions; these affiliations should not be interpreted as making CIAW an administrative unit of those institutions. Journal-specific policies, submissions and publication terms are available at AI & Antiquity.
